# Acceptance matrix

Scope: the original macOS delivery is joined by the iOS/iPadOS MVP started on 2026-09-13. Mobile simulator results are recorded separately from physical streaming and distribution acceptance; tvOS remains future work. A playable slice is not release completion. PASS requires executed evidence; statuses are PASS, FAIL, SKIP, BLOCKED or TODO.

The [clean offline baseline](validation/validation-baseline-2026-09-12.json) passed 36 Swift tests. The [pairing follow-up](pairing-fix-validation.md) passed its 39-test suite (24 XCTest and 15 host tests) and visibly verified saved pairing plus 48 app launch buttons in the relaunched app at the user's tailnet address. The agent did not enter the PIN. The later [stream follow-up](stream-deadlock-validation.md) verified short HEVC 10-bit Desktop playback, native full-screen/windowed launch, local capture/release and disconnect, and corrected a live drawable lock inversion. Composite host and streaming requirements remain BLOCKED until their remaining checks are executed.
